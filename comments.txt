Norrbotten
The "landskap" of Norrbotten was officially recognized in 1995, though the name has long been used to describe the portion of Västerbotten situated within Norrbottens län (Norrbotten County). The county itself was established in 1810. Consequently, the border between the provinces of Norrbotten and Västerbotten follows the modern county line.

The border with Finland shifted in 1809 when Russia gained control of Finland and Torneå. This boundary follows the Torne and Muonio rivers, and with a few marked border cairns near the twin cities of Haparanda and Tornio.

Regarding the borders with Lappland (specifically the Torne, Lule, and Pite lappmarks), historical records are sparse. For the most part, the province line follows modern municipal boundaries with a Modern Deviations removed: Small bends near Lake Juntikkajärvi, Nuolivuoma, Ollikainen, and Innerträsket appear to be modern administrative adjustments. Historical borders were typically defined by straight lines between cairns or followed natural features like streams. Notably, the bend around Ollikainen does not appear on the Häradskarta (historical map).

The coastal border follows the limit of Swedish territorial waters in the Bay of Bothnia.

The border with Lycksele Lappmark generally follows modern municipal boundaries. It begins at Virvasröset on the Norwegian border and follows a line of cairns to Slagnäs. Modern administrative bends near Slagnäs and Lill-Nartok have been removed, as they do not appear on the Generalstabskarta or Häradskarta. From Slagnäs onward, the border follows the Skellefte River (Skellefteälven). Note that in 1869, Malå parish/socken was transferred from Norrbotten County to Västerbotten County, which historically suggests it may have been considered part of Pite Lappmark.

Regarding the border with Västerbotten, several modern deviations have been corrected based on historical maps: The modern bend around Gallejaur is not present on older maps; therefore, the border is drawn as a straight line through the area, A small modern deviation near Glommersträsk has been removed to align with historical records.

The boundary with Åsele lappmark is not a historical landskap border and generally follows modern municipal boundaries, with specific exceptions near Risträsk where it follows the straighter lines shown on the Stomkarta and older Generalstabskarta. The modern bend around Alsberget has been removed so that Alsberget remains within Lycksele lappmark, consistent with the Stomkarta and Generalstabskarta. The border is also straightened near Malmed at the corner of the Åsele and Vilhelmina parishes, with the deviation around Telegnäset removed.

This border differs from modern municipal boundaries near Bellvik, Norråker, and Gåraträsksjön up toward Jämtland; it follows the straighter historical lines on the Stomkarta, placing Bellvik in Ångermanland and Gåraträsksjön in Åsele lappmark.

The border towards Jämtland follows the Saxån river up to Sutme, continuing along the Slipsiksån river to Lake Slipsiken and the cairn before Riksröse 204. Although the area around Lillånäs west of Rajastrand is currently part of Norrbottens län due to recent administrative changes, it historically belongs to the landskap of Jämtland and is mapped accordingly.
